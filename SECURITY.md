# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability
Do not publish exploit details, credentials, customer data, or payment information in a public issue. Contact the repository owner through a private channel configured for the project. A private reporting address has not yet been published; until it is, request a private contact from the owner and avoid sharing sensitive details in public issues.

## Scope
This policy covers HOLBERY Marketplace code/configuration. HIO production and Bozq One System are separate and must not be tested or modified through this repository.

## Expectations
Provide a concise description, impact, affected commit/version, and safe reproduction steps. Allow time for triage before public disclosure. Do not access or retain data that is not yours. Do not test payment systems or third-party platforms without authorization.
