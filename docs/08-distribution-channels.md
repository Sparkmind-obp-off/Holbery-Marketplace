# Distribution and Channel Strategy

## Principle
Maintain one canonical product record and asset source, then adapt listings to each channel. All listings must remain truthful and consistent.

## Channel candidates
- HOLBERY storefront: canonical brand and catalog home.
- Shopee Indonesia: only product formats and listing types permitted under current seller policies.
- TikTok Shop: only supported product/service formats and compliant delivery mechanisms.
- Etsy: consider if product type, seller eligibility, fees, tax, and country support fit.
- Other channels: add only after a documented case.

No channel is approved, integrated, or live until current requirements and account eligibility are verified.

## Channel listing record
Track channel, seller account identifier (never credentials), listing URL, SKU, status, channel-specific title/description/price/deliverables, policy-check date/source, last verification date, operator, fees, delivery method, support, and refund handling.

## Publishing workflow
1. Validate demand and product quality.
2. Review intellectual-property rights and licenses.
3. Confirm platform rules and seller eligibility.
4. Adapt the listing without misleading claims.
5. Calculate net revenue after platform/payment fees.
6. Publish manually until official API automation is supported and tested.
7. Test buyer experience and delivery.
8. Monitor questions, refunds, and policy notices.

## Avoid
Spam, fake reviews, fabricated scarcity or sales counts, copied listings, restricted-data scraping, unlicensed assets, and guaranteed-results claims. Do not bulk-post without policy checks. Search snapshots are not proof of current sales.
