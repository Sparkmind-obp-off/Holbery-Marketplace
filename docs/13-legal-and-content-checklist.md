# Legal, Licensing, and Content Checklist

This is an operational checklist, not legal advice.

## Before public launch
- Confirm legal seller identity and details that must be displayed.
- Publish terms, privacy notice, support contact, and refund/cancellation policy suitable for the product and channel.
- Review consumer protection, tax, invoicing, data protection, and digital-content obligations.
- Confirm the platform permits the product type and delivery method.
- Record ownership/licenses for fonts, icons, photos, illustrations, code, datasets, and templates.
- Avoid third-party trademarks that imply affiliation.
- Include required attribution and notices.
- Review claims; do not promise guaranteed earnings or outcomes.
- Provide appropriate notice/consent for marketing and analytics.

## Asset rights record
For every asset, record creator/source, license, permission evidence, attribution, commercial-use status, modification rules, and date checked. If rights are unclear, do not publish.

## AI-assisted work
Review correctness, originality, rights risks, privacy leakage, and usefulness. Do not submit confidential/customer data to a model without an approved basis. A human reviewer must approve final deliverables.
