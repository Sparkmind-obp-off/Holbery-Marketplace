# Release Checklist

## Product and content
- [ ] Target customer and job-to-be-done are specific.
- [ ] Promised deliverables exist and work.
- [ ] Version, formats, compatibility, license, support, and update policy are documented.
- [ ] Instructions and examples are tested.
- [ ] Asset rights are documented.
- [ ] No fake testimonials, fake sales claims, or misleading guarantees.

## Application
- [ ] Catalog validation passes.
- [ ] Build, lint, type-check, and tests pass.
- [ ] Mobile, keyboard, and accessibility checks pass.
- [ ] Error, empty, and not-found states work.
- [ ] No secrets or personal data in code, logs, or client bundles.
- [ ] Published/unpublished visibility is tested.
- [ ] Preview and production environments are separate.

## Sales and delivery
- [ ] Channel eligibility/current policy checked and recorded.
- [ ] Net margin after fees reviewed.
- [ ] Buyer checkout and delivery tested end-to-end.
- [ ] Refund/support workflow works.
- [ ] Payment is verified server-side if first-party checkout is used.
- [ ] Paid assets are not publicly accessible without authorization.

## Approval
- [ ] Owner approved copy, price, policy, and release.
- [ ] Rollback plan documented.
- [ ] Evidence attached to PR or release notes.
- [ ] Status reflects what was actually verified.
