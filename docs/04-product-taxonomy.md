# Product Taxonomy and Portfolio

## Categories
1. Business & Finance: bookkeeping, cash flow, inventory, pricing/HPP calculators, sales reports.
2. Productivity & Operations: planners, trackers, SOPs, checklists, workflow kits.
3. Creative & Design: original editable templates, catalogs, menus, promotional kits, social assets.
4. Knowledge & Education: practical guides, workbooks, tutorials, training materials.
5. Software & Tools: calculators, dashboards, small web tools, business utilities.
6. AI & Automation: prompt kits, workflow designs, spreadsheet automation, reusable agent instructions.
7. Industry Solutions: online sellers, micro-retailers/warungs, service businesses, barbers, creators.
8. Bundles & Premium Systems: interoperable products with a clear outcome.

## Product ladder
- Free sample: demonstrates quality and validates interest.
- Entry product: narrow task and clear deliverable.
- Core product: complete workflow with instructions and examples.
- Bundle/system: multiple interoperable assets with setup guide and support boundaries.
- Optional service add-on: fixed scope, delivery time, and capacity limits.

## Required product record
ID, slug, SKU, title, category, status, short/full description, target customer, job-to-be-done, deliverables, formats, language, compatibility, price/currency, license, version, preview assets, delivery method, support/update policy, validation evidence, rights review, quality review, channel listings, and timestamps.

## Lifecycle
Idea → evidence collection → prototype → user test → quality/rights review → listing draft → owner approval → published → maintain, pause, or archive.

## Quality bar
Each product needs a usable deliverable, instructions, versioning, and examples where useful. Do not publish generic unreviewed AI output, copied templates, pirated software, unlicensed assets, or unverifiable claims.

## Initial hypotheses, not validated
- Multi-outlet consignment and settlement workbook.
- Micro-seller inventory and cash-flow workbook.
- Simple pricing/HPP calculator.
- Original editable promotional catalog templates.
- Small-business SOP bundle.
- Narrow automation tool with a demonstrable time-saving outcome.

Rank ideas by evidence, urgency, willingness to pay, reachability, production effort, and support burden before scaling.
