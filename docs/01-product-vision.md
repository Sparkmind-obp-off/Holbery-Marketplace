# Product Vision — HOLBERY Marketplace

Status: Draft for owner approval | Version: 0.1.0 | Updated: 2026-10-21

## Vision
Build a reliable product-owned storefront and operating workflow where HOLBERY can create, validate, publish, sell, deliver, and improve original digital products across multiple channels.

The first release is a first-party HOLBERY store, not a multi-vendor platform. This keeps scope, support, product quality, and operating costs manageable while demand is unproven.

## Customer promise
- Practical products that solve a defined job.
- Clear contents, compatibility, limitations, and instructions.
- Honest previews and product descriptions.
- Reliable access to purchased deliverables through a verified channel.
- Support and update policies stated before purchase.

## Business objectives
1. Validate demand before investing in complex infrastructure.
2. Reach the first real paid order with a small useful product and compliant channel.
3. Build reusable product assets and distribution workflows.
4. Track views, clicks, conversion, refunds, support load, and repeat purchases where lawfully available.
5. Keep fixed costs near zero until revenue supports them.

## Non-goals for the first release
- Multi-vendor onboarding, seller payouts, or commissions.
- Replacing Shopee, TikTok Shop, Etsy, or a payment processor.
- Unverified claims of integrations, sales, demand, or automated delivery.
- Publishing AI output without human quality and rights checks.
- Touching HIO production or the private Bozq One System.

## Success measures
- One product with a defined customer and job-to-be-done.
- Evidence of interest with dates and source links.
- One complete purchase/delivery test in an approved channel before launch.
- No exposed secrets or known critical security issues.
- Product economics documented: price, fees, support time, refunds, and net revenue.

## Principles
Evidence before assumptions; ship small; privacy by design; reversible technical decisions; no paid dependency until justified; label status accurately as planned, in progress, verified, or blocked.
